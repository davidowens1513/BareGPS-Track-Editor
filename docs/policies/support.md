# Support

Updated: 12 November 2026

BareGPS Track Editor helps you edit GPX tracks and combine them into a walking route to save or share. The BareGPS suite of apps is home at https://appsbyonzy.dev.

## Contact

For help, bug reports or feature requests, email appsbyonzy@gmail.com. Support is handled by a solo developer, so response times may vary.

Please include:

- Your device model and iOS, Android or browser version.
- The Track Editor version, what you were trying to do and the steps leading to the problem.
- Any error message and a screenshot, if it does not reveal information you wish to keep private.

Do not send passwords, access keys or private tracks unless needed and you are comfortable sharing them.

## Open and edit tracks

Open Track imports GPX files. Use Open another Track? or Open files under Input Tracks to add more. Inputs remain visible together; the stronger line and Current label identify the input you are editing. Tap another line or its Input Tracks row to select it. Tap the current line for handles, then drag to reshape a section. Done editing hides the local handles.

Use Trim before/after, Reverse, Add points, Insert point or Delete point as needed. Reset Track restores the input’s retained starting version; Undo reverses an edit. Your original GPX file is not overwritten.

## Simplify a detailed track

Simplify previews a lighter copy while retaining the original. Accept simplified copy makes the new input current. If you add that copy to Route Plan, its simplified geometry is used in the confirmed route and exported GPX. Cancel makes no changes.

If a file or plan is too large, try simplifying or trimming an input, or work with smaller sections. The app explains when an operation exceeds its limits.

## Build and save the route

Add to Route Plan saves a copy of the current input. Reorder, remove or replace entries in Route Plan, then Confirm Plan to assemble the output. Later input edits do not automatically change plan copies. Save Route exports the confirmed route as GPX.

Keep the original files and exported copies of important work. Local autosave is recovery, not a full backup or cloud sync. Clearing app or browser data or uninstalling can remove local work; exported copies must be managed separately.

## Maps and heights

If map tiles do not load, check your connection and tap Retry. Online map and terrain services may be unavailable.

Fetch Terrain fills missing heights and refreshes changed sections while keeping existing valid recorded heights. Fetch before adding the input to Route Plan, or replace an existing plan entry after refreshing. Confirm Plan also fetches heights still needed for the finished route. If a request fails, retry, cancel, or explicitly choose Confirm without terrain where offered. u/a means heights are unavailable or need refreshing. Heights, ascent and descent are estimates.

## Location

The location button requests a current position after permission. If it fails, check device or browser location settings. Location permission is optional for editing tracks. Track Editor does not record walks or continuously track your location in the background.

## Settings and Route Cards

Settings selects your preferred coordinate display, enables point naming and changes input colours. With no input open, a colour choice sets the default for new imports. GPX coordinates are always stored in the standard format supported by GPX.

The Route Card lists checkpoints along the confirmed route. Full Card shows every point. Expand the card to share text or PDF. Check the chosen destination before closing the share sheet.

## Data and privacy

Tracks and preferences are primarily stored locally, using operating-system app or browser protections. There is no separate app password or encrypted route vault. Protect your device and review precise coordinates and names before sharing an export.

Map requests reveal the area being viewed to OpenTopoMap. Terrain lookups send coordinates to Open Topo Data, through a Cloudflare relay in the browser version. See the Privacy Policy for details and choices.

## Outdoor use

Check access, terrain, weather and route suitability independently. Lines connect points directly and do not automatically follow roads or paths. Routes and heights are planning estimates. Track Editor is provided as is and is not a navigation, surveying or emergency service.
