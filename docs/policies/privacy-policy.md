# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

BareGPS Track Editor imports, edits, simplifies and combines GPX tracks into walking routes. It displays estimated terrain heights and lets you save or share GPX files and Route Cards. This policy covers the iPhone and Android apps and the browser version.

The BareGPS suite of apps is home at https://appsbyonzy.dev.

## Information used by the app

Track Editor uses imported GPX coordinates, supported point names, heights and timestamps, input and route names, colours, Route Plan copies and app preferences. Inputs, reset starting versions, the plan, confirmed output and preferences are saved locally on your device or in your browser when storage is available. No account or sign-in is required.

Opening a GPX file does not upload the complete file to a developer-operated route service. Online maps and terrain lookups make the requests described below.

## Location

If you grant permission and tap the location button, the app requests your current position to centre the map. It does not record your movements or run continuous background location tracking. You can decline or revoke permission in device or browser settings and still import and edit tracks.

## Storage and your choices

Autosave is recovery, not cloud sync or a complete backup. Keep original GPX files and export important routes. Editing does not overwrite the original GPX file. Reset Track restores an input’s retained starting version; it does not delete plan copies, confirmed output or exported files. Removing an input or clearing a route does not remove files you previously exported.

Clearing app or browser data or uninstalling may remove local work, subject to operating-system backup and restore behaviour. Files chosen from cloud storage and exported or shared copies are managed by the storage service or destination you select.

## Maps and terrain services

Online maps are requested from OpenTopoMap when the map loads or you move or zoom it. Requests reveal the map tiles and area being viewed. These can indicate an approximate or precise location, including the area around your current position if you use the location button. The provider also receives ordinary internet request information, such as your IP address, request time and technical details sent by your device or browser. Map data attribution includes OpenStreetMap contributors and SRTM.

Fetch Terrain sends route and sampled coordinates to Open Topo Data to obtain estimated ground heights. Confirm Plan can also fetch missing heights for the assembled route. Track and point names are not included in terrain requests. On iPhone and Android, these requests go directly to Open Topo Data; in the browser version they pass through a Cloudflare-hosted relay. Cloudflare also hosts the website and browser app.

Map, terrain and hosting providers may retain request details in access, error, security or backup logs, including IP addresses and requested map areas or coordinates. We do not assume these requests are anonymous or deleted immediately. We have not verified a fixed retention or backup-deletion period for OpenTopoMap or the public Open Topo Data service, and cannot promise when those providers delete records. Requests may be associated with network identifiers such as an IP address. Each provider handles information under its applicable practices and may use hosting or delivery services.

The developer does not operate a route account or cloud route database. Do not fetch terrain or confirm a plan that needs terrain for coordinates you do not want sent to these services. Local work can still be edited without a connection, but online maps and height requests may be unavailable.

Provider information: https://opentopomap.org · https://www.opentopodata.org · https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

## Saving, sharing and external links

Saving writes GPX to a destination you select. Sharing a Route Card or GPX sends it to the app or recipient you choose. Exports may contain precise coordinates and names you entered. Check them before sharing. The destination handles those copies under its own terms. Opening website, support or policy links uses the relevant website service. The published support and policy pages are hosted by Notion.

## Support

If you email appsbyonzy@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and attachments. This information is used to answer your request, investigate problems and maintain the app. Include only information you are comfortable sharing. Ask for deletion of support correspondence using the same address; some copies may remain in email backups or where retention is required.

## Advertising, sale and tracking

Track Editor does not sell personal information, use advertising SDKs or include third-party analytics SDKs. It does not use your tracks or location for advertising or cross-app tracking. Normal operational handling by map, terrain, hosting, device and sharing services is described above.

## Data security

The app relies on operating-system app protections and browser storage protections for local information. Map and terrain connections use HTTPS. Tracks and exports are not protected by an additional app password or an app-managed encrypted vault. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device and take care when sharing precise coordinates.

## Children

Track Editor is not directed specifically to children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information through support or direct contact, please contact the developer.

## Changes and contact

This policy may change when the app or its services change. The current version will be published here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or requests, email appsbyonzy@gmail.com.
